OUR STORY

Building a Family Legacy

NTE Construction is a residential renovation company based in Johannesburg and Pretoria, Gauteng.

Founded in 2015 by James Teffo, the company started as a small family business and has grown through its commitment to quality workmanship and customer satisfaction.

Today, NTE Construction is controlled by Director Lesetja Teffo, the son of James Teffo, who continues the family's commitment to reliable and personalised renovation services.
